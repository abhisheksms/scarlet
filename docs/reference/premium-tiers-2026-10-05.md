Read on 2026-10-05. Scope: public pages only (Play listings, developers' own sites and help centres, review aggregators). No sign-in, no install, no download of any app or APK, nobody contacted. Features are described in my own words. This file holds facts and sources, not a recommendation.

## How the facts were read (read this before trusting a cell)

- **Play listings** were read with India parameters (`hl=en_IN&gl=IN`). The web-fetch tool could not render these pages (about 1.2 MB each; it returned only a truncation notice), so I read the raw HTML from the shell and parsed it in memory. Nothing was saved or installed. The Play web page also embeds roughly 20 recent reviews per app, with developer replies, beyond the 3 shown in a text view; those are the source of the complaint lines in Part 3 and of several pricing statements made in developer replies.
- The listing's **In-app purchases line is only a minimum-to-maximum range across every item**; items are not named, so a range cannot tell a one-time purchase from a subscription. The ads and in-app-purchase labels are the badges shown under the developer name. Install brackets are printed as the India listing prints them (1Cr+ = 10,000,000+, 1L+ = 100,000+, 1B+ = 1,000,000,000+).
- **Developer sites** were read through the fetch tool (its summaries). Where a point mattered I re-fetched asking for verbatim text (JustBlocked pricing, Gingko Lab, Calls Blacklist, Whoscall). Prices on developer sites came back in USD or GBP because the fetch ran outside India; rupee prices on those sites were not read.
- **Blocked or failed**: `truecaller.com` and `support.truecaller.com` ("unable to fetch" from the tool; I did not work around it), `support.hiya.com` (HTTP 403), `ryosoftware.com` (403), `apps.lithiums.ru` (connection refused), Trustpilot page for whoscall.com (404).
- **Evidence grades.** A = read on the Play listing, or on the developer's own site or help centre (I also count the developer's own App Store listing as A, marked iOS). B = a third-party review aggregator (Trustpilot, Sikayetvar); user-submitted, so it shows what people say, not what is true. C = a blog or marketing page. A statement that comes from a reviewer on a Play listing is graded A for *what the page shows* but is labelled reviewer-stated, because the reviewer may be wrong.
- Search-engine summaries were used only to find pages; no cell rests on a summary I did not open.

## Platform facts used for the verdicts in Part 2 (Android reference, read 2026-10-05, grade A)

- https://developer.android.com/reference/android/telecom/CallScreeningService (page footer: last updated 2026-09-16 UTC)
  - The service is bound just before an incoming call rings and must reply within 5 seconds; the phone does not ring until it replies or the time runs out. It may use local lookups. It is given the role through the call-screening role request (RoleManager).
  - It is handed only five properties of the call: direction, the carrier's number-verification status, connect time, creation time and the handle (the number). Everything else is empty.
  - Only calls with a tel: handle are passed; only calls from numbers that are not in the user's contacts are passed unless the service holds the contacts permission; calls whose caller presentation is restricted, unknown, unavailable or payphone are not provided to it.
  - The reply can allow, silence or disallow the call; replies are ignored unless the call is incoming (outgoing calls are visible for caller-ID purposes only). Blocked or rejected calls are written to the system call log with a block type and a call-screening block reason.
- https://developer.android.com/reference/android/telecom/CallScreeningService.CallResponse: the reply object has flags to disallow (API 24), reject (API 24), silence (API 29), skip the call-log entry (API 24) and skip the notification (API 24).
- https://developer.android.com/reference/android/telecom/Call.Details (page footer: last updated 2026-08-28 UTC): `getHandle` returns the number (API 23); `getCallerNumberVerificationStatus` (API 30) reports whether the network could verify the number under the STIR process of ATIS-1000082, and "not verified" means nothing is known.
- A screening-role app that holds no contacts permission is therefore handed exactly the calls the founder's app acts on (numbers not in contacts), with the number, time and direction, and nothing about contacts, the call log, SMS or phone state.
