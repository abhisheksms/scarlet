# 00 — Product vision

## What it is

An Android app with one job: **calls from numbers that are not in your contacts do
not interrupt you.** One switch chooses between off, silence (the call comes through
without a ringtone) and block (the call is rejected). Everything the app handles is
listed in a history on the phone.

## Who it is for

People in India, first, who get several telemarketing, loan and "KYC" calls a day and
have stopped answering unknown numbers anyway. They searched the store for "block
unknown calls" or "spam call blocker" on a mid-range phone. They do not want another
app that reads their contacts and their call log, and they will not make an account.

## Why it wins

1. **It needs nothing private.** Android only hands a call-screening app the calls
   from numbers outside the user's contacts. So the app never asks for contacts, the
   call log or an account, and can say so truthfully.
2. **It never costs a call that mattered.** Contacts always ring. A number that calls
   back within minutes can ring. Filtering can be paused for a delivery or a cab.
   Chosen numbers can be allowed for good. If anything goes wrong inside the app, the
   call rings.
3. **The screen holds still.** Ads sit in reserved space; nothing loads late and
   slides a row out from under a thumb. State is readable in one glance.
4. **Rules a person can read.** The decision for a call is the first matching line
   of a short ordered list. Phase two adds lines (the first two, India's 140 and 160 number series, landed on 4 October 2026),
   not a new engine.

## What it is not

Not a caller-ID service, not a spam database, not a dialer. It does not know who is
calling; it knows the caller is not in your contacts.

## How it earns

Ads in phase one, shown through AdMob behind Google's consent flow. Phase two adds a
one-time Pro unlock through Google Play. No subscription, no consumables.

## Tone

Terse and adult. Buttons in Title Case, body text in sentence case. The app states
what it will do and what it did. No welcome tour, no coaching copy, no congratulating
the user. Numbers shown are real counts from the phone's own log.
