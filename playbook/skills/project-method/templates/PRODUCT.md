## Accessibility & Inclusion

Launch-blocking, not polish: full TalkBack/VoiceOver support with a specified
narration order per screen; every control has a role and label; color-state
always paired with text or shape; WCAG-AA contrast on all text;
`prefers-reduced-motion` respected everywhere.
