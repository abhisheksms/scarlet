## Full description (≤ 4000 chars)
```
Cricket Auction Simulator puts you in the franchise war room on mega auction day. One purse. 182 players. Nine rival franchises who want exactly what you want. When the hammer falls, the squad is yours — and so is the season that follows.

There is no countdown clock. The pressure is the auctioneer's call — "Going once… going twice… SOLD!" — on live, unpredictable timing. Any bid resets the call. Rivals bait bidding wars, snipe at the death, storm back into contests they had left, and hold grudges when you snatch their man.

THE AUCTION
• A marquee set first, then five role batches — 182 players, every one biddable
• Real auction economics: a ₹125 crore purse, tiered bid increments, a ₹30 crore ceiling on any single player
• Squad rules with teeth: 18–25 players, maximum 8 overseas
• A live feed of bids, drop-outs and comebacks — all from real, legal bids, never faked

THE WAR ROOM
• Scout the full player pool and build a private watchlist before the auction
• Filter by role, sort by ability, star up to 50 targets
• Inspect any franchise mid-auction: their signings, spend, purse and squad gaps — the auction waits while you read the room

THE PAYOFF
• A full post-auction season: standings, playoffs, a champion, your finish
• Rivalries that carry across games, and a squad card worth sharing

BUILT FOR THE LONG HAUL
• Plays fully offline — no account, no waiting
• Two save slots, pause and resume any time
• Easy and Hard modes with genuinely different rival behaviour

Free to play with optional rewarded ads, used only to fast-forward a batch — never during live bidding. One optional purchase removes all ads forever and grants zero gameplay advantage.

All franchises and players are fictional. No real names, teams or league branding.
```

- **Content rating (IARC questionnaire):** target **Everyone / PEGI 3**. Answer

  simulated gambling**. Nothing is ever staked on chance: bidding is a player

  has real-world value either.) Yes to "users can purchase digital goods".
