# Founder taste

Read `FOUNDER_TASTE.md` in this folder in full before UI, copy or monetization
work. It is the founder's own calls, each anchored in their words, and it is the
same file in every Cyan Harbor project: this copy is the master. The one-line
version: **build like FC Mobile, price like you mean it, talk to adults, and
never fake anything.**

## The twelve sections, as a checklist

1. **Mission:** revenue through craft. "Addictive" means mastery, variety, payoff
   and replayability, never manipulation.
2. **No AI slop:** no welcome or tutorial screens, no chummy narrator copy, no
   placeholder graphics, no goofy invented names, no labels that restate the
   screen, and the word "AI" never in player-facing text.
3. **Talk to adults:** terse, confident copy; when in doubt delete the sentence.
   Title Case on buttons and CTAs, sentence case in body text. Teach
   diegetically, never through overlays.
4. **Screen economics:** live state stays visible; hierarchy, not cramming. Test
   on a cheap, small phone. Before deleting information, check where else it
   lives.
5. **Realism as texture:** copy reality's rules, never its names, logos, colours
   or kits. Adjacency is taste; imitation is a takedown.
6. **Opponents compete:** rivals are aggressive, competent and hold grudges.
   Emotion is derived from real state, never fabricated.
7. **Immersion over instrumentation:** the founder tests by playing on a real
   phone. Optimise time-to-device; build stamps so a stale install can't lie.
8. **Monetization:** price with self-respect, one honest product, friction
   allowed but deception never, every paid control states its cost first.
9. **Naming and distribution:** the category keyword is the name, because that
   is what the audience types. Mass-market India. Studio brand Cyan Harbor,
   package prefix `com.cyanharborstudios.*`, no self-branding inside the product.
10. **Ruthless subtraction:** if the ecosystem provides it, delete it; every
    subtraction records what would bring it back.
11. **Polish and performance are the vibe:** 60 fps on a budget device in a
    profiled release build; short choreographed splash; motion communicates
    state; accessibility is polish.
12. **Working with the founder:** outcomes first, honest numbers, initiative on
    the reversible, respect a parked decision.

## How to apply it

- Copy: write the terse version first, then cut again. If a label restates what
  the screen already shows, remove it.
- Layout: one place, once. Core play screens never scroll.
- Monetization: the price shown always comes from Google Play. Never a countdown,
  never urgency copy, never an ad triggered by a loss or scarcity. Keep a test
  that asserts dark-pattern copy is absent.
- Names: fictional, plausible, close to the real thing. Run the policy checker's
  trademark and real-people lists over new names.
- When the founder makes a new taste call, add it to `FOUNDER_TASTE.md` here,
  with their words as the anchor and the date, so every project gets it.
